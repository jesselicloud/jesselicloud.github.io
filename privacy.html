Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Morning Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own video recordings to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What Google data it uses

The app requests one permission: YouTube upload access (youtube.upload). It uses this only to upload video files that the owner has placed in a folder on their own computer, and to set those videos to private. It does not read, change or delete any other YouTube or Google account data.

How data is stored

The sign-in permission (an OAuth token) is saved only on the owner's own computer. Video files are sent directly from that computer to YouTube. The app has no server, and nothing is sent to anyone other than Google.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
